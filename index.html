01

Overview

More flexible routing can come with a cost: attention sinks, extreme activations, and sensitivity to quantization.

AttnResidual introduces depth-wise attention alongside token-wise attention. With standard softmax, both must allocate all probability mass to real tokens or branches—even when the desired update is close to zero.

OASIS gives each router an explicit null channel through Softmax1. Its key addition is token-to-depth null coupling: aggregate the null probabilities across attention heads and use the resulting signal to adjust depth-routing logits. A branch with stronger null evidence is downweighted relative to one with weaker null evidence.

The paper studies this mechanism theoretically under stated assumptions and evaluates activation statistics, language-model perplexity, and reasoning accuracy under quantization.

02

Inside OASIS

Connect what the token router knows to what the depth router decides.

  1. 1

    Make room for a null state

    Softmax1 adds 1 to the normalization denominator, leaving probability mass for an explicit zero-valued option.

  2. 2

    Summarize null evidence

    Aggregate token-level null probabilities across heads to obtain a branch-level signal.

  3. 3

    Couple tokens to depth

    Center that signal and inject it into the depth logits before null-aware depth normalization.
